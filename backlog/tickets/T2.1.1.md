## Description
Implement scripts/download_data.py (Kaggle API, or printed manual instructions) and src/shelflife/data/favorita.py. Select 12 SKUs with perishable=1 across 4-5 families (e.g. DAIRY, DELI, PREPARED FOODS, BREAD/BAKERY, EGGS) using a written rule (e.g. top volume among items non-zero in >=95% of weeks). Aggregate daily to weekly (Mon-Sun) and across all stores (a DC-level proxy). Handle: missing calendar days (e.g. 25 Dec absent in the source), negative sales (returns: clip to 0 and log count), fractional units (weighed items), partial first/last weeks (drop). Do not commit raw data. Include a short EDA notebook.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] data/processed/demand_weekly.parquet has columns sku_id, item_nbr, family, week_start, units; exactly 12 SKUs; no NaN; contiguous weeks per SKU; >=200 weeks per SKU
- [ ] SKU selection rule is written in the notebook and reproduced by code; selected item_nbr list saved to configs/skus.yaml
- [ ] pytest checks: schema, contiguity, non-negativity; rebuild is deterministic (file hash equal on two runs)
- [ ] notebooks/01_demand_eda.ipynb shows per-SKU time plot, seasonality (week-of-year) and CV / zero-share table
- [ ] `git status` shows no raw data committed; docs/assumptions.md records the 'retail sales as DC demand proxy' assumption
